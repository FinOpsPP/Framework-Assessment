# 38: Collect Business Drivers Regularly

**Specification Version:** 1.9.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 38-collect-drivers

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Collect business drivers (launches, promos, growth) on a set schedule.

## Supplemental Guidance

In order to create accurate forecasts, some subject-matter-expert intuition is required. For example, a national
gym studio might ramp up web and database resources in January in expectation of new years resolution sign ups.
While this may show up as seasonality in historical data, the business is usually already aware of this change.
Similarly, engineering teams may have a deployment schedule where they create resources to support new features,
but if this info isn't shared with FinOps, it won't make it into the forecast. Thus, it's important for FinOps
to work with multiple stakeholders to determine what are the major business drivers of cost for their company.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Collect data based on existing patterns observed in spend
* Conduct proactive investigations and interviews with teams
* Gather existing self reporting by the business and/or teams
* Factor in expected increases to the forecast model
