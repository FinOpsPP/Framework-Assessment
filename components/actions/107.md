# 107: Map Invoices To Allocation Model

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 107-map-invoices

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Map provider invoices to the allocation model and financial systems (GL, ERP).

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Ledger codes are often mapped first
because Finance needs them to post anything at all, long before anyone writes down which invoices
arrive or when.

No coverage rate applies. A provider invoice typically arrives monthly, so a year is a list of twelve and an
assessor reads them rather than sampling.

Item 5 is often skipped, because reporting the lines that resolve to nothing means naming
spend with no owner. A practice that maps 80% of its invoice and reports the other 20% is in better
shape than one that maps 80% and leaves the rest.

The capability spans
all technology spend, and an invoice from a software vendor needs the same mapping as one from a cloud
provider.

Action 13 in the Cost Allocation capability measures unallocated resource cost, which is a different
layer. This action is about the invoice reaching the ledger, so item 5 counts invoice lines that map to
no cost center rather than resources that carry no tag.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every provider invoice received during the review period. Invoices arrive on a monthly
cycle and are few enough to enumerate, so items are assessed against each one.

* Intake defined (which provider invoices arrive, on what cycle, and who receives them)
* Allocation mapping in place (invoice lines resolve to a cost center through the allocation model)
* Ledger codes mapped (each cost type carries the general ledger code Finance posts it to)
* Format and timing agreed (the output matches what accounting needs and arrives before close)
* Unmapped lines reported (invoice lines that resolve to no cost center are reported in currency)

10*Ceil(x/5)
