# 102: Define Technology Financial Policies

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 102-draft-policies

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Define technology financial policies, the risk each reduces, and who approved them.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Approval is often recorded long
before anyone writes down which scopes a policy covers.

No coverage rate applies to the items. A policy set is a list you can print, so an assessor reads each
policy. A percentage would round hard on a set of three.

This action scores the quality of the policy record, not how much of the estate the policies reach. A
practice with one well-documented policy can score 10 here. Coverage is scored by action 103 through
guardrail reach, and adherence by action 105.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every policy in force during the review period. A policy set is small enough to
enumerate, so items are assessed against each policy rather than as a coverage rate.

* Policy register (policies in force are listed in one place, each with a named owner and an effective
  date)
* Risk named (each policy records the financial risk it exists to reduce)
* Scope stated (each policy records which FinOps Scopes and technology categories it applies to)
* Approval recorded (each policy records who approved it and at what level of authority)
* Next review dated (each policy carries the date by which it is reviewed again)

10*Ceil(x/5)
