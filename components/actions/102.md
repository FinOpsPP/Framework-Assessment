# 102: Draft Cloud Financial Policies

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 102-draft-policies

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Draft clear, goal-aligned cloud financial policies (what "good" looks like).

## Supplemental Guidance

The items are unordered because a policy library is usually improved one attribute at a time across every
policy at once, rather than one policy at a time. Score against the policy set, not the best example in it.

Item 4 is the item that separates a policy from a preference. "Resources should be tagged appropriately"
cannot be assessed, enforced, or argued with. "Every resource carries the four required tags" can be all
three, and gives action 105 something to monitor.

Item 5 exists because a policy with no sanctioned way to deviate is a policy that gets quietly ignored.
An exception that never expires is the same failure with better paperwork.

Item 6 is the discipline the security field learned first. A policy written for a team lands as a rule
imposed on them, and a policy without a review date accumulates until nobody can say what is still in force.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Each policy states the outcome it protects and the business or FinOps goal it serves
* Each policy names an accountable owner and the approver who signed it off
* Each policy defines its scope: the accounts, environments, technology categories, and personas it binds
* Each policy defines "good" in terms that can be measured, rather than in statements of intent
* Each policy defines its exception path, including who grants an exception and when it expires
* Policies are drafted with the teams they bind, and held in version control with a stated review date
