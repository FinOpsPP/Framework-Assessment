# 34: Establish Scenario Decision Process

**Specification Version:** 1.9.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 34-scenario-process

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Establish decision process for which scenarios feed the forecast.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though a practice usually
gathers deployment plans before it works out how to time them into the forecast.

Item 3 keeps a forecast from drifting upward. Optimization work lowers the run rate
on its own schedule, and a forecast that only adds new projects will sit above reality until
somebody reconciles it by hand.

Action 37 records the forecasting approach and action 39 scores how often the forecast is refreshed.
This action covers the decision process that chooses which scenarios go into it. A practice can hold
a well documented forecasting approach and still feed it whatever plans arrived by email.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the projects and optimization work feeding the forecast during the review period. These
are few enough to enumerate, so items are assessed against each one rather than as a coverage rate.

* Define and follow process for gathering deployment plans from teams (the process names who submits
  plans, on what cadence, and in what form)
* Determine timeline for each new project to adjust the forecast during that time (each project carries
  start and ramp dates, so its spend lands in the periods it occurs in)
* Determine how optimizations and lower run rate will impact the forecast on a rolling basis (committed
  optimization work is carried into the forecast as a reduction on a stated schedule)

10*Ceil(x/3)

### Scoring Details
