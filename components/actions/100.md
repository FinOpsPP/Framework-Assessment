# 100: Leverage Vendor Training Resources

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 100-leverage-vendor

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Leverage CSP/vendor training; contextualize to your environment.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. A catalogue usually exists as a
list of links long before anyone works out who pays for the paid courses.

Item 3 is the whole point of the action. Vendor training teaches the vendor's product in general,
and a practitioner finishing it still does not know which of those services this organization runs,
which are forbidden, or where the approval path is. A link with no note is a reading list.

Item 4 exists because vendor catalogues move. A course retires or is replaced on the vendor's
schedule rather than yours, and a list nobody rechecks sends people to a page that no longer loads.

No coverage rate applies. A catalogue is a list you can read, so an assessor checks the entries
rather than sampling them.

Action 98 covers the curriculum the practice writes itself. This action covers the training somebody
else wrote and what the practice adds around it. Action 101 measures completion and whether people
come back to the material, including this material, so uptake is not scored here.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the vendor and provider training the practice points people to during the review
period.

* Catalogued per persona (the vendor training relevant to each persona is listed, with its cost and
  the effort it takes)
* Access arranged (people can reach the training without seeking approval each time, and who pays
  is recorded)
* Contextualized (each listed course carries a note on what applies here, including which services
  the organization actually runs and which parts to skip)
* Catalogue rechecked (the list is reviewed on a stated cadence, because vendors retire and replace
  courses without telling anyone)

10*Ceil(x/4)
