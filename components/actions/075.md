# 75: Integrate Billing With Procurement

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 75-integrate-billing

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Integrate billing + entitlement data with Procurement/ITAM/SAM.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Named counterparts usually come
first, because the data arrives by asking someone before it arrives on a schedule.

Item 3 makes the capability measurable. Spend without entitlement counts
cannot produce a utilization rate, so a practice missing this item can report what it paid and not
whether it used what it bought.

Item 4 is where the data sits rather than who owns it. Asset management teams hold the contract
and license position, and a practice that keeps usage data to itself leaves them reconciling by
email.

Action 1 in the Data Ingestion capability covers inventorying data sources generally. This action is
narrower, covering the license and SaaS feeds and the entitlement data that has to arrive with them.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the license and SaaS billing and entitlement feeds in use during the review period.

* Retrieved without a person (billing arrives on a schedule rather than being downloaded by hand)
* Normalized schema (billing arrives in a common format such as FOCUS rather than per vendor
  exports)
* Entitlements joined (purchased quantities sit alongside the spend, so utilization can be computed)
* Shared with asset management (contracts, usage and spend are available to the IT and software
  asset management functions rather than held inside FinOps)
* Named counterparts (Procurement, Finance and asset management each have a named contact)

10*Ceil(x/5)
