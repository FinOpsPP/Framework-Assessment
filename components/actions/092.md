# 92: Create Implementation Roadmap

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 92-implement-roadmap

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Create an implementation roadmap and prioritized backlog.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Plenty of practices run a backlog
with owners and dates long before anyone writes down how it gets ordered.

Item 3 decides whether the roadmap survives a change of leadership. A backlog ordered
by whoever asked most recently is not effective, and cannot be handed over.

No coverage rate applies. A backlog is a list you can print, so an assessor reads the items rather
than sampling them.

Action 116 in the FinOps Assessment capability publishes an improvement roadmap built from assessment
results. This action covers the roadmap for building the practice itself: the tooling, the hires, and
the capability rollout. A practice can have a strong assessment roadmap and no plan for its own build.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the roadmap in force during the review period, and the items on its backlog.

* Roadmap published (a roadmap for building the practice exists where the personas it affects can
  read it)
* Items carry an owner and a date (each backlog item names who is accountable and when it is due)
* Prioritization stated (the rule used to order the backlog is written down, so two people ordering
  it reach the same result)
* Dependencies recorded (items blocked by another capability, a tool, or a hire say so)
* Progress reviewed (completed and slipped items are reviewed on a stated cadence with a named owner)

10*Ceil(x/5)
