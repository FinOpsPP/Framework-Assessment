# 94: Publish Finops Kpis

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 94-publish-finops

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Publish KPIs (adoption, tagging compliance, anomaly response, savings rate).

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Most practices publish numbers
long before they publish what the numbers mean.

Item 3 is often overlooked, because reporting the value of the FinOps team invites the
question of whether it is worth its cost. The Framework asks for it anyway, and a practice that
cannot show what it generated is hard to fund through a lean year.

Item 5 turns a dashboard into a report. A tagging compliance figure that fell four points
with no explanation gets read as noise, and the next person stops looking.

Action 49 selects the KPIs and defines the measurement scope, and action 16 gathers the
persona-specific requirements behind them. This action scores publishing them. A practice can pick
excellent KPIs and score nothing here by keeping them in a spreadsheet.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the KPI set the practice publishes during the review period.

* Published on a cadence (the KPI set is issued on a stated schedule rather than on request)
* Reaches beyond the practice (the personas each KPI concerns receive it, not only the FinOps team)
* Value of the practice included (at least one KPI reports the savings or cost avoidance the
  practice generated)
* Definitions travel with the numbers (each KPI states what it measures and over what period)
* Movement explained (a change in a KPI is published with the reason, not only the new figure)

10*Ceil(x/5)
