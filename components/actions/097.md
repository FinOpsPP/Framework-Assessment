# 97: Assess Skills & Training Needs

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 97-assess-skills

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Run needs/skills gap assessment across personas and teams.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Most practices know their gaps
informally long before anyone writes down what each persona is supposed to understand.

Item 4 stops this becoming a survey nobody acts on. A list of gaps with no order produces
content about whatever the author finds interesting, and the persona with the widest gap is rarely
the one who asked loudest.

No coverage rate applies. A persona list is short, so an assessor reads it rather than sampling.

Action 91 records the skills the FinOps team itself needs and the gaps in them. This action covers the
personas the practice depends on across the organization: engineering, finance, procurement, product
and leadership. A practice can have a fully skilled FinOps team and no idea what anyone else knows.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the personas the practice depends on during the review period.

* Personas listed (the personas the practice depends on are named, not assumed)
* Need recorded per persona (what each one has to understand to play its part is written down)
* Current level recorded (what each persona knows today is assessed against that need)
* Gaps ranked (the gaps are ordered, so the next content produced has a stated reason)
* Reassessed on a stated cadence (the assessment is repeated on a schedule with a named owner)

10*Ceil(x/5)
