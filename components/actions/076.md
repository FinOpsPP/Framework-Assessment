# 76: Validate Utilization & Remediate

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 76-validate-utilization

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Validate utilization/compliance; remediate idle/over-provisioned licenses.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. A compliance check usually comes
first because it is the one an auditor asks for, and reclamation arrives later because it means
taking something away from a team.

Item 3: without a written threshold, reclaiming a license is an
argument every time, and the team that argues hardest keeps the seat it does not use.

Item 4 scores the route and the record, not a count. A period where nothing needed reclaiming earns
it by recording that decision against each entitlement. A practice with no way to reclaim does not.

The inactivity window in item 2 is yours to set and has to be written down. Thirty days of silence
means something different for a daily reporting tool than for software used at quarter end, and an
unwritten window means the answer changes with whoever runs the report.

Action 78 publishes the utilization rate. This action acts on it. A practice can publish an accurate
40% every month and score nothing here if no license ever comes back.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every license and SaaS entitlement held during the review period.

* Compliance checked (resources requiring a license are checked against the entitlements held, and
  the shortfall or surplus is recorded)
* Idle assignment identified (assigned licenses with no activity are identified against a stated
  inactivity window)
* Reclamation threshold stated (the usage level at which a license is reclaimed or reassigned is
  written down)
* Reclamation path exists (a license below the threshold can be reclaimed or reassigned through a
  stated route, and each review records what was reclaimed, reassigned, or kept)
* Triggers fire without a person (reclamation starts from the usage data rather than from somebody
  remembering to look)
