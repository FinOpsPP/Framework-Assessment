# 98: Develop Role-Based Learning Content

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 98-develop-role

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Develop role-based curricula, playbooks, and onboarding content.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though organization specific
content usually arrives after someone notices that the generic material does not explain how
chargeback works here.

Item 3 decides whether the material gets used twice. Generic FinOps content explains
the concepts and stops at the point where somebody has to act, because acting depends on which tools
and which approval path this organization has.

Item 5 looks like administration and is why content goes stale. A curriculum with no named owner is
nobody's to update when the tagging policy changes.

Action 127 in the Automation, Tools & Services capability covers training users on a specific tool
and putting data in their path. Action 104 covers enabling stakeholders on policy. This action covers
the standing curriculum behind both.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the personas the practice depends on during the review period.

* Material exists per persona (each persona has content aimed at it rather than one general deck)
* More than one level (content exists beyond an introduction, so somebody can go further)
* Organization specific content included (material covers how FinOps works here, not only generic
  concepts)
* Onboarding carries it (new employees and contractors receive the material relevant to their
  persona when they join)
* Owner named (each curriculum records who maintains it)

10*Ceil(x/5)
