# 95: Drive Stakeholder Engagement

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 95-drive-stakeholder

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Drive stakeholder engagement; make wins and misses visible.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order except for items 3 and 4
which come in a pair. Named contacts usually come
first, since the rest is hard without knowing who to talk to.

Items 3 and 4 are deliberately a pair. A practice that publishes only wins is doing marketing, and
the engineers it depends on will read it that way. Publishing a missed target in the same place is
what makes the wins believable.

Regarding item 5, a persona who raises a question and never hears back stops raising them,
and the practice loses the signal before it notices the silence.

Action 104 covers enabling stakeholders on policy specifically, and the FinOps Education capability
covers training. This action is about the standing relationship rather than any single message.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the personas the practice works with during the review period.

* Named contacts (each persona the practice depends on has a named counterpart on both sides)
* Regular contact (engagement happens on a stated cadence rather than when something breaks)
* Wins published (savings and avoided cost are reported with the team that earned them named)
* Misses published (targets that were missed are reported in the same place as the wins)
* Requests tracked (a request to the practice carries an owner and a response, so a persona can see
  what happened to it)

10*Ceil(x/5)
