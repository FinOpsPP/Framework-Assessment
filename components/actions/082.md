# 82: Track Roi & Notify Owners

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 82-track-roi

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Track ROI, coverage gaps, expirations; notify owners early.

## Supplemental Guidance

Reporting and notifying are scored together on purpose. A report nobody reads and an alert with no
supporting number both fail the same way, which is that the owner cannot act on what they received.

Item 2 asks for coverage broken out by owner, not only in total. An organization sitting at healthy
aggregate coverage can still hold a business unit that is entirely on demand, and only the attributed
view surfaces it.

Lead time in item 5 should be long enough for the owner to actually decide, which in most organizations
means at least one full planning cycle rather than a notice in the final week of the term.

The measures reported here are the same ones scored in action 81. This action scores whether they reach
the accountable owner in time to change a decision, not whether the portfolio is healthy.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Report commitment return on a recurring schedule using effective savings rate or an equivalent measure
* Report coverage against commitment eligible spend, attributed to the owners accountable for that spend
* Report commitment waste as unused commitment cost over total commitment cost
* Maintain an inventory of every commitment with its expiration date and remaining term
* Notify accountable owners ahead of expiration with enough lead time to renew, resize, or let it lapse
* Alert owners when coverage or utilization crosses a defined threshold, rather than only at period close
