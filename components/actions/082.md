# 82: Track Roi & Notify Owners

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 82-track-roi

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Track ROI, coverage gaps, expirations; notify owners early.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Expiry reminders are usually the
first thing a practice builds, since a lapsed commitment is immediately expensive, and return reporting
often arrives much later.

No coverage rate applies to the items. A commitment portfolio is a list you can print, so an assessor
checks each commitment rather than computing a percentage. A rate would round hard on a portfolio of
four.

Items 1 and 2 ask whether the numbers are reported, not whether they are good. A practice returning 3%
still earns item 1 by reporting it. Whether the return is acceptable is a purchasing decision, and
action 81 scores that through its savings rate and purchase criteria.

Items 3 and 5 name their thresholds somewhere other than the alert itself, on purpose. A practice that
picks its own trigger will pick one that never fires, and a practice that sets its own notice period
will set one too short to act on. Item 3 reads the level from the commitment strategy that action 79
scores, so the two actions have to agree. Item 5 uses one full purchase cycle, taken from the cadence
in action 81, because the only notice worth giving is notice long enough to decide and buy.

If nothing expires during the review period, items 4 and 5 are assessed against the register and the
notification process rather than against a notice that was never sent. An empty expiry list is not a
failure, and it is not a free pass either.

Utilization appears in this action and in action 81, and they measure different things. Action 81 scores
the level the portfolio reaches. This action scores whether somebody finds out when that level drops. A
practice can sit at 92% and still have no alerting.

Action 80 scores building the analysis that produces these numbers. This action scores reporting them
and telling the people who can act. A practice with a strong analysis nobody reads earns 80 and not
this one.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every commitment held during the review period, and every commitment expiring in it.
A portfolio is small enough to enumerate, so items are assessed against each commitment rather than as
a coverage rate.

* Return reported (the return on each commitment is reported in currency or as an effective savings
  rate)
* Coverage reported (the share of eligible usage covered by commitments is reported for the period)
* Decline alerting (an alert fires to a named recipient when utilization falls below the level
  recorded in the commitment strategy)
* Expiration register (every commitment carries an expiry date and a named owner)
* Advance notice (owners of expiring commitments are notified at least one full purchase cycle before
  expiry)

10*Ceil(x/5)
