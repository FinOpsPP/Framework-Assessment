# 109: Design Chargeback With Finance

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 109-design-chargeback

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Co-design chargeback/showback with Finance; document rules and cadence.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though most practices document
allocation rules first and discover the correction path the first time a charge lands on the wrong
cost center after close.

Item 1 makes this action different from writing a chargeback design alone. The
Framework treats the decision on whether shared costs stay central or get allocated by consumption as
a joint one between FinOps and Finance, and a rule set Finance has not signed is a proposal.

Item 3 matters because showback and chargeback carry different obligations. Showback can run at any
granularity and is always worth doing. Chargeback posts to a budget somebody is accountable for, so it
depends on accounting policy and cannot be switched on by the FinOps team deciding to.

Item 5 is about the route existing, not how often it is used. A practice with no corrections in the
period earns it by having the path documented. A practice that handles corrections by emailing Finance
each time does not.

This action scores the design. Action 110 scores automating the runs, action 111 scores distributing
reports and tracking accuracy and disputes, and action 112 scores resolving the variances and widening
shared cost coverage. A practice can hold an excellent documented design and score poorly on all three.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Finance approval recorded (Finance is named in the chargeback rules and has signed off on them)
* Allocation rules documented (which cost types reach which cost centers, including shared costs and
  commitment discounts)
* Showback and chargeback scope stated (which scopes are shown for information and which post to
  official accounting budgets)
* Cadence documented (when the run happens relative to financial close, and when cost centers receive
  their figures)
* Correction path documented (how a posted charge is corrected after close, including true-up entries)

10*Ceil(x/5)
