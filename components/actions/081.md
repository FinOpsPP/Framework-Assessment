# 81: Purchase Commitments Regularly

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 81-purchase-commit

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Purchase commitments frequently; target healthy utilization.

## Supplemental Guidance

CRAWL (0-14) Commitments are bought once a year, or whenever someone remembers. Utilization and coverage
are not measured, so nobody can say whether the last purchase was a good one.

WALK (15-30) Purchases happen on a quarterly rhythm. Utilization is tracked and sits in a defensible
range, but coverage of eligible spend is still thin or measured only for compute.

RUN (31-42) Monthly purchases against a reviewed pipeline. Utilization holds above 90% and coverage
reaches most eligible spend across services, not just the one service that was easiest to model.

FLY (43-50) Purchasing is continuous or automated against a laddered portfolio. Utilization stays at or
above 95% and coverage is high enough that the remaining on demand spend is a deliberate choice.

Utilization carries the heaviest weight because an unused commitment is worse than no commitment. Buying
frequently while utilization falls is the failure mode this weighting is meant to expose.

Coverage is judged against commitment eligible spend, not total spend. Counting spend that no commitment
could ever cover understates a portfolio that is working as intended.

A high coverage target is not automatically the right one. Workloads with a short remaining life or an
architecture change in flight are correctly left on demand, and action 79 is where that reasoning is
documented so that a lower coverage score can be read as a decision rather than a gap.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Weighted sum
  Purchase cadence (score x3),
  Commitment utilization (score x4),
  Coverage of eligible spend (score x3).
  Max raw (50)

* Purchase_Cadence - How often the portfolio is reviewed and incremental purchases are made
  (0=Never, 1=Ad-hoc, 2=Annual, 3=Quarterly, 4=Monthly, 5=Weekly or automated)
* Commitment_Utilization - Used commitment divided by total commitment over the period
  (0=Not measured, 1<70%, 2=70-79%, 3=80-89%, 4=90-94%, 5=95%+)
* Eligible_Coverage - Share of commitment eligible spend covered by a commitment discount
  (0=Not measured, 1<25%, 2=25-49%, 3=50-69%, 4=70-84%, 5=85%+)

* Output Range (0-50)

### Scoring Details

**Score Type:** multi_bucket

| Score | Condition |
| ----- | --------- |
| 0 | Low Maturity, Score Range (0-14) |
| 4 | Developing, Score Range (15-30) |
| 7 | Established, Score Range (31-42) |
| 10 | Optimizing, Score Range (43-50) |
