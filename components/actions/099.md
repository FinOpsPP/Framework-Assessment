# 99: Offer Certifications & Informal Learning

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 99-offer-certification

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Offer certifications and informal learning (office hours, lunch-and-learns).

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Informal sessions usually start
first, because they cost nothing but an hour.

Item 1 asks whether the route is funded and who qualifies, not how many people hold a certificate. A
practice that will pay for certification but has never said so has not offered it, because nobody
asks for a budget they do not know exists.

Regarding item 4, certification budgets tend to sit with the FinOps team
and the engineers who make the daily spend decisions never see the offer.

Action 98 covers the standing curriculum. This action covers what is offered on top of it: the
certifications and the sessions people can choose to attend.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the learning opportunities offered during the review period.

* Certification route exists (a funded path to FinOps certification is offered, and who is eligible
  is written down)
* Informal sessions run on a stated cadence (office hours, lunch and learns or equivalent, on a
  schedule rather than when somebody volunteers)
* Opportunities counted (the number offered in the period is recorded)
* Open beyond the practice (allied personas can attend, not only the FinOps team)

10*Ceil(x/4)
