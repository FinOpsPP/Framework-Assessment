# 111: Distribute Reports & Track Accuracy

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 111-chargeback-report

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Distribute chargeback reports; track accuracy, cycle time, and disputes.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Reports usually go out long before
anyone measures how late they are.

Item 4 asks for the accuracy figure to travel with the report. A practice that computes accuracy and
keeps it internally is grading its own work, and a recipient who cannot see it has no way to judge
whether a charge is worth checking.

Item 5 scores the record, not the count. A period with no disputes earns it if the route and the
recording exist, and a practice with no way to capture a dispute outcome does not. Zero disputes can
mean the charges are right or that nobody believes challenging them will change anything.

Action 112 scores the accuracy level the practice achieves. This action scores whether recipients are
told what it is. A practice can run at 1% variance and still score poorly here by never publishing it.

Action 109 documents the correction path, which is the accounting route for fixing a posted charge
after close. Item 5 here is the service side of the same story: what a recipient raised, and what
happened to it.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every chargeback report issued during the review period, and every dispute raised
against one.

* Recipients named (each cost center has a named recipient for its report)
* Distribution tied to close (reports reach recipients on a stated schedule relative to financial
  close)
* Cycle time measured (the time from close to report delivered is measured against a stated target)
* Accuracy published with the report (recipients see the accuracy figure alongside their charges
  rather than it being held internally)
* Dispute outcomes recorded (each dispute records what was challenged, how it resolved, and whether
  the charge changed)

10*Ceil(x/5)
