# 91: Define Org Model & Budget

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 91-org-model-budget

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Define org model, staffing/skills, and operating budget for FinOps.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. A practice often has a funding
source before anyone writes down the operating model, because the budget came with the first hire.

Item 1 uses the capability's three shapes rather than the words centralized, federated, and hybrid.
Those are common in the wider industry but the Framework describes a single central team, a fully
virtual team drawn from Finance and Engineering, and a hub and spoke model, so those are the terms
used here.

Item 4 asks about the source, not the amount. A practice funded generously out of whatever budget had
room this year is less stable than one funded modestly from a line that renews, and only the second can
plan past the current period. The Framework's Run tier calls this a regular funding source.

A fully virtual team can score 10. Item 2 asks which support is virtual rather than hired, not that
roles be filled by headcount, because the Framework names virtual support as a legitimate shape rather
than a shortfall.

Action 96 reviews practice maturity on a cadence. This action covers the shape, staffing, skills,
funding, and decision model of the team itself, which the Framework treats as a separate review of the
team's needs, size, roles, and placement.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Operating model recorded (which shape the team takes, whether a single central team, a fully virtual
  team, or a hub and spoke with members embedded in Engineering, and where it reports)
* Staffing plan recorded (the roles the practice needs, requested against filled, and which support is
  virtual rather than hired)
* Skills mapped (leadership, communication, analytical, and technical skills mapped to the roles, with
  the gaps named)
* Funding source named (the team has a standing budget line rather than a one-off allocation)
* Decision model documented (how a cross-functional or urgent decision gets made, and who makes it)

10*Ceil(x/5)
