# 77: Influence Workload Design Choices

**Status:** Accepted

**Implementation Types:**

## Descriptive Statement

Influence workload design (IaaS vs PaaS vs SaaS) with cost/compliance input.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though a team usually supplies a
license position before it can compare anything.

Item 2 names three shapes because the cost ordering between them reverses depending on what the
organization already owns. A workload that looks cheapest as a managed service can be cheaper on
plain infrastructure when an existing license covers it, a the comparison is the only way to know.

Item 3 is not a formality. Some license terms restrict deployment by processor count, by tenancy, or
by hosting provider, and a design that breaches them creates an exposure that surfaces at audit
rather than at deployment.

Action 61 scores the architecture review process itself, including its trade-off model and who
approves a placement decision. This action scores the licensing input into that review. A practice
can run an excellent review that never asks what it already owns.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every workload design reviewed during the period that consumes licensed software.

* License position supplied (the review receives what the organization already owns and what it
  would need)
* Options compared (the same workload is costed under infrastructure with an existing license,
  a managed platform service, and a SaaS product)
* Compliance checked (the design states whether the license terms permit the deployment)
* Decision recorded (the option chosen and the licensing reason behind it are written into the
  design record)

10*Ceil(x/4)
