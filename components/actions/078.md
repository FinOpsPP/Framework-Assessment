# 78: Publish License Spend & Utilization

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 78-publish-license

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Publish license and SaaS spend and utilization to stakeholders on a stated cadence.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Spend is almost always published
before utilization, because spend arrives on an invoice and utilization has to be assembled.

Item 3 is what makes the report actionable. Spend alone says what was paid; utilization says whether
it bought anything. A team at 40% on a renewing agreement has a decision to make, and a team reading
only the spend figure does not know that.

Regarding item 4, Procurement and Finance receive these numbers by default, and
the team actually holding the unused licenses is the one that can release them.

Action 94 publishes the practice's own KPI set. This action publishes license and SaaS spend and
utilization specifically, to the people who hold the agreements.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the license and SaaS reporting issued during the review period.

* Published on a stated cadence (the report is issued on a schedule rather than on request)
* Spend included (license and SaaS spend for the period, split by agreement)
* Utilization included (assigned licenses divided by purchased licenses, as a rate)
* Reaches the holders (the teams holding the licenses receive it, not only Procurement and Finance)
* Renewal horizon shown (agreements approaching renewal are listed with their dates)

10*Ceil(x/5)
