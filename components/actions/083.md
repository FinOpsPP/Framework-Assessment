# 83: Allocate Upfront Costs Transparently

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 83-allocate-upfront

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Allocate upfront costs appropriately and transparently.

## Supplemental Guidance

The order follows the only path that works. A treatment has to be chosen before it can be applied, applied
before cost can be attributed, attributed before rules are worth publishing, and published before a
reconciliation dispute has anything to settle against.

The point of item 2 is that no owner should see a one time spike they did not cause. An unamortized
upfront charge landing in a single month makes that owner's trend meaningless and their forecast wrong.

Item 3 covers the benefit as well as the cost. An organization that charges amortized commitment cost to
the consuming team while holding the discount centrally has allocated the bill without allocating the
reason anyone agreed to it.

Unused commitment cost is the hard case, which is why item 4 names it. Whether it is socialized across
all consumers or held against the team that requested the commitment, the rule has to be written down
before the first unused month, not after.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

1. Decide and document how upfront commitment charges are treated: amortized over the term, or recognized
   at purchase
2. Apply that treatment consistently in the cost data used for showback and chargeback
3. Attribute the amortized cost, and the discount benefit it produces, to the owners whose usage consumed it
4. Publish the amortization and benefit sharing rules where owners can read them, including how unused
   commitment cost is handled
5. Reconcile allocated commitment cost back to the invoice each period and report any residual left over

10*Ceil(x/5)
