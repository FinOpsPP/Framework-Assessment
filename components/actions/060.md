# 60: Communicate Unit Cost Wins

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 60-communicate-wins

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Communicate wins (unit cost reductions) to drive cultural adoption.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Most practices name a currency
figure long before they name the unit that moved.

Item 2 separates a unit economics win from a savings announcement. Spend falling by
two hundred thousand could mean the architecture got cheaper or the business got smaller, and only
the unit metric separates those two.

Item 3 exists because the three figures are not interchangeable. Avoidance is cost that never
arrived, savings is cost that fell, and a reduction can be either. Reporting one total invites a
finance partner to look for money that is not in the budget.

The capability describes unit costs as guiding a cultural shift rather than only reporting one,
which is item 5. A win delivered where engineers already work changes behavior; the same
win in a monthly finance pack does not reach them.

Action 95 publishes the practice's wins and misses generally. This action is narrower, covering unit
cost improvements and the evidence behind them. Action 55 defines the units and action 56 documents
how they are calculated.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every unit cost improvement communicated during the review period.

* Evidence recorded (each win records the baseline, what was changed, and the outcome)
* Unit named (the win states which unit metric moved, not only a currency amount)
* Measurement type separated (avoidance, savings and cost reduction are reported as distinct
  figures rather than one total)
* Team credited (the team whose change produced the result is named)
* Delivered per persona (each audience receives it in the channel it already uses)
