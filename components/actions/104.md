# 104: Educate Stakeholders On Policy

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 104-educate-policy

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Enable/educate stakeholders on policy expectations and exceptions.

## Supplemental Guidance

The items are unordered, but items 5 and 6 are the ones most often missing. A program that cannot say who
has been reached, or whether being reached changed anything, is running on faith.

Item 2 is what shifting left looks like for policy. A check in a pull request, a field in a ticket
template, or a message in the console at the moment of the decision reaches an engineer that a wiki page
never will.

Item 6 has to be read carefully. A rise in exception requests after enablement is usually a good sign,
because it means teams learned the sanctioned path exists. A rise in violations is the bad sign.

Reach in item 5 is measured against the personas the policy binds, which is defined by item 3 of action
102. A policy with no defined scope cannot have its reach measured.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Policy expectations are part of onboarding for every persona a policy binds
* Enablement is delivered where the work happens, not only in a document that has to be sought out
* The exception path is taught beside the rule, so teams know the sanctioned way to deviate
* Enablement is refreshed whenever a policy changes, and the change is announced to the affected owners
* Reach is measured as the share of in scope owners who have completed the current enablement
* Effect is measured, using violation counts and exception volume as the signal that enablement worked

10*Ceil(x/6)
