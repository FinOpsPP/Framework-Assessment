# 104: Educate Stakeholders On Policy

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 104-educate-policy

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Enable stakeholders on policy expectations, exceptions, and who accepts the risk.

## Supplemental Guidance

Item 1 is met by reaching the people a policy binds, not by volume of communication. A policy published
only where the FinOps team reads it has not been communicated, however well written it is.

The Framework asks leaders to accept accountability for risk decisions within their authority, and a
waiver with no named acceptor is a risk the organization is carrying without knowing whose it is.
Recording the name is also what makes the waiver reviewable later.

No coverage rate applies. Policies and the personas they bind are both short lists, so an assessor
checks the pairs rather than computing a percentage.

Action 101 in the FinOps Education & Enablement capability scores training breadth and whether content
is refreshed. This action is narrower, covering only what a policy requires of the people it binds. A
practice can run excellent general enablement and still fail every item here.

Item 4 looks like administration and is where most practices lose the score. Ownership changes far more
often than policy does, and a policy set communicated once decays as the people it bound move on.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every policy in force during the review period, and the personas each one binds.

* Expectations delivered (each policy has been communicated to the personas it binds, not only to the
  FinOps team)
* Exception path communicated (the personas a policy binds know how to request an exception, who
  decides, and how long one lasts)
* Risk acceptance recorded (where a policy is waived or a breach is tolerated, the leader accepting
  that risk is named in the record)
* Handover covered (a new owner receives the policies that bind their scope when they take ownership)

10*Ceil(x/4)
