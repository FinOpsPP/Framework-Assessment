# 105: Monitor Compliance & Report Breaches

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 105-compliance-report

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Monitor compliance and report breaches, along with remediation paths.

## Supplemental Guidance

This is a threshold rather than a bucket because the stages replace one another. A practice whose
breaches are remediated automatically is not also reporting them to somebody who fixes them by hand,
so asking it to tick the lower rungs would score its history.

Action 103 scores guardrails, which stop a breach at the moment of the decision. This action scores
what happens to breaches that occur anyway, whether because no guardrail covers that case or because
one was overridden. Prevention and detection are different controls and a practice needs both; a
high score here with a low score on 103 means the practice is good at cleaning up after itself.

The gap between reported and manually remediated is where most practices sit. A breach report with
no owner and no expected fix becomes a standing list that grows, and the report stops being read
once it is long enough.

Automated remediation at stage 10 means the fix runs without waiting for a person, not that nobody
reviews it. A remediation that can delete or resize something an owner depends on needs the same
exception path action 103 scores, or it gets switched off the first time somebody reacts to it.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Scored at the highest stage the practice reaches for policy breaches. The stages replace one
another: a practice remediating automatically is not also waiting to be told.

Measured over: the policies in force during the review period and the resources they bind.

### Scoring Details

**Score Type:** threshold

| Score | Condition |
| ----- | --------- |
| 0 | Not monitored |
| 2 | Reported |
| 5 | Manual remediation |
| 10 | Automated remediation |
