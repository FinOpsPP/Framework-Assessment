# 26: Record Anomaly Context & Timeline

**Specification Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 26-record-anomaly

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Record each anomaly with context, scope, impact, and timeline.

## Supplemental Guidance

The items are unordered because a team can start from any field. They are all about the record itself,
not about how fast the team responds. Detection, notification, and resolution times are scored by
action 28, which can only be measured once the timeline in item 4 is being captured.

The 90% threshold, rather than 100%, accepts that a small number of records will be opened late or
closed without a full field set. Below 90% the field is not reliable enough to compute a KPI from, so
the item is not met. The rate is computed over the full review period rather than sampled.

Retention is set at 13 months so that a period can be compared with the same period one year earlier.
A shorter window makes year over year comparison impossible and hides seasonal recurrence.

Without a recorded disposition there is no denominator for a false
positive rate, which is what action 29 tunes against.

The record does not need a dedicated tool. An existing ticketing or incident system is usually the
better choice, because it already carries ownership, history, and retention. This tool should not
be email, chat, or spreadsheet, because those are not queryable and do not have a single source of truth.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Denominator: all anomalies detected during the review period. A field item is met when the field is
populated on 90% or more of those records.

* Single system of record
* Scope fields populated (billing account, service, region, and a named accountable owner)
* Impact fields populated (variance amount in the reporting currency, and the start and end of the
  period the variance covers)
* Timeline fields populated (first occurrence, detection, notification, and resolution timestamps)
* Disposition field populated (true, expected change, or false)
* Retention and queryability (records retained for at least 13 months and queryable by scope,
  disposition, and root cause)

10*Ceil(x/6)
