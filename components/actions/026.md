# 26: Record Anomaly Context & Timeline

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 26-record-anomaly

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Record each anomaly with context, scope, impact, and timeline.

## Supplemental Guidance

The items are unordered because a team can start from any field. They are all about the record itself,
not about how fast the team responds. Detection, notification, and resolution times are scored by
action 28, which can only be measured once the timeline in item 4 is being captured.

Item 5 matters more than it looks. Without a recorded disposition there is no denominator for a false
positive rate, which is what action 29 tunes against.

The record does not need a dedicated tool. An existing ticketing or incident system is usually the
better choice, because it already carries ownership, history, and retention.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Capture every detected anomaly in a durable record rather than a chat thread or an inbox
* Record the affected scope: billing account, service, region, and the accountable owner
* Record the impact as a variance amount in currency, along with the period the variance covers
* Record the timeline: first occurrence, detection, notification, and resolution timestamps
* Record the disposition: true positive, expected change, or false positive
* Retain records so they stay queryable across periods and recurring anomalies can be found
