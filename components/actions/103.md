# 103: Publish Guidelines & Guardrails

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 103-publish-guideline

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Publish technology guidelines and the guardrails and automation that enforce them.

## Supplemental Guidance

CRAWL (0-14) Guidelines exist somewhere a determined person could find them. Breaches are noticed in
the invoice, if at all, and exceptions are whatever somebody agreed to in a meeting.

WALK (15-30) Guardrails cover some routine decisions and warn the requester. Guidelines are published,
though mostly to the FinOps team rather than to the engineers making the decisions.

RUN (31-42) Guardrails cover most routine decisions and block a breach rather than reporting it,
guidelines are reachable from the tools where decisions get made, and exceptions carry expiry dates.

FLY (43-50) Guardrails remediate drift without waiting for a person, guideline changes are announced to
the personas they bind, and an exception is either renewed with a reason at expiry or it lapses.

Coverage carries the heaviest weight because an unenforced guideline is a suggestion. Enforcement mode
is next because coverage alone can be entirely advisory: a practice can route 90% of decisions through
a guardrail that only ever writes a log line.

The two together are the honest measure. Coverage without enforcement is theater, and enforcement
without coverage protects a corner of the estate while the rest proceeds unchecked.

The exception path is weighted lightest but is not optional. A guardrail with no way around it gets
switched off the first time it blocks something urgent, and then coverage drops to zero without anybody
recording a decision.

Action 102 scores defining the policies. This action scores publishing them and enforcing them. Action
105 scores monitoring adherence and reporting breaches, so the share of spend that actually complies is
measured there rather than here.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

* Weighted sum
  Guardrail coverage (score x4),
  Enforcement mode (score x3),
  Guideline publication (score x2),
  Exception path (score x1).
  Max raw (50)

* Share of routine technology decisions that pass through an automated guardrail
  (0=Not measured, 1<20%, 2=20-39%, 3=40-59%, 4=60-79%, 5=80% or higher)
* How a guardrail responds when a decision breaches policy
  (0=No guardrails, 1=Reported after the fact, 2=Warns the requester, 3=Warns and notifies the owner,
  4=Blocks the breach with an override path, 5=Blocks and remediates the drift automatically)
* Where guidelines are published and who can reach them
  (0=Unpublished, 1=Shared on request, 2=Published to the FinOps team,
  3=Published to the personas each guideline binds, 4=Reachable from the tools where decisions are
  made, 5=Versioned, with changes announced to the personas they bind)
* How an exception is requested and bounded
  (0=No exception path, 1=Granted informally, 2=Requested and recorded, 3=Approved by a named role,
  4=Carries an expiry date, 5=Reviewed at expiry and either renewed with rationale or withdrawn)

* Output Range (0-50)
