# 101: Track Training Impact & Refresh Content

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 101-track-training

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Track participation and effect on KPIs; refresh content on a stated cadence.

## Supplemental Guidance

The description asks for a stated cadence rather than refreshing on cadence, because a cadence
nobody wrote down is a cadence nobody misses. How often depends on how fast the material goes stale,
and content about a tagging policy ages the day the policy changes.

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Completion is almost always
measured first, because the platform that serves the material counts it for you.

Items 1 and 2 measure different things and a practice can be strong at one and weak at the other.
A completion rate near 100% says the material was required. People returning to it later says the
material was useful, which is the harder result and the one that predicts whether behavior changed.

Item 4 scores the route and the record, not a count of updates. A period where the material was still
accurate earns it by recording that decision. A practice with no review path does not.

Item 3 deliberately does not name which KPI. The right one depends on what the training was for, and
the capabilities those KPIs belong to score the figures themselves. This action asks only that the
training be reported against one.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the personas the practice depends on, and the learning material published to them.

* Completion measured (the share of each persona that finished the material intended for it)
* Consultation measured (how often teams return to the material after the session that introduced
  it)
* Effect on a named measure reported (training is reported against a KPI it was meant to move, such
  as tagging compliance or forecast accuracy)
* Refresh path exists (material is reviewed on a stated cadence, and each review records what was
  updated, retired, or kept)

10*Ceil(x/4)

### Scoring Details

**Score Type:** bucket

| Score | Condition |
| ----- | --------- |
| 0 | No items completed |
| 3 | 1 item completed |
| 5 | 2 items completed |
| 8 | 3 items completed |
| 10 | 4 items completed |
