# 106: Iterate Policies Based On Feedback

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 106-iterate-policies

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Iterate policies based on outcomes and stakeholder feedback.

## Supplemental Guidance

The order holds because each step supplies what the next one needs. There is no review without a cadence,
no decision without evidence, no change to publish without a decision, and nothing to measure without a
published change.

Item 2 is where the rest of this capability pays off. Compliance rates come from action 105, exception
volume from the exception path in action 102, and stakeholder feedback from the enablement channels in
action 104.

Retiring a policy is a legitimate outcome and is scored the same as amending one. A policy library that
only grows becomes impossible to enable against, and the guardrails that enforce it are never free.

Item 4 names the guardrails on purpose. A policy amended without updating its enforcement leaves the
published rule and the enforced rule saying different things, which is the drift that action 103 closes
at its highest score.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

1. Set a review cadence for every policy and record the next review date on the policy itself
2. Gather evidence for the review: compliance rates, exception volume and age, violations, and feedback
3. Record an outcome for each policy reviewed, being one of keep, amend, or retire
4. Version the change, publish it with its rationale and effective date, and update the matching guardrails
5. Measure the effect at the next review, and retire policies that no longer earn their enforcement cost

10*Ceil(x/5)
