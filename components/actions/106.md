# 106: Iterate Policies On Outcomes & Risk

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 106-iterate-policies

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Iterate policies on adherence outcomes, realized risk, and stakeholder feedback.

## Supplemental Guidance

A review driven only by who complained loudest will tighten the policies that annoy people and leave
the ones quietly costing money, because nobody complains about a policy they are ignoring. That is why
item 2 asks for outcome data as an input rather than treating feedback as the only signal.

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order. Most practices start with a cadence
and a feedback inbox, and only later connect the adherence and breach numbers that make the review
worth holding.

Item 2 names guardrail false positives on purpose. A guardrail that blocks legitimate work is a policy
problem wearing a tooling costume, and it will be routed around rather than reported unless the review
asks for the number.

Item 5 scores the route and the record, not a tally. A practice whose policies are all still needed
earns it by recording that decision, and a practice with no way to withdraw one does not. Without the
route a policy set only ever grows, and every stale rule is something a guardrail still enforces and an
engineer still works around.

Action 105 scores monitoring adherence and reporting breaches. This action scores whether those reports
change anything. A practice with excellent compliance reporting that never amends a policy earns 105
and not this one.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every policy in force during the review period, and every change made to one.

* Review cadence published (the policy set is reviewed on a stated cadence with a named owner)
* Outcome data feeds the review (adherence rate, breach counts, and guardrail false positives are
  inputs to it rather than reported separately)
* Feedback path open (the personas a policy binds can raise an issue through a stated route, and
  those issues reach the review)
* Change recorded (each policy change records its rationale and effective date)
* Retirement path exists (a policy can be withdrawn through a stated route, and each review records
  which policies were kept, amended, or withdrawn)

10*Ceil(x/5)
