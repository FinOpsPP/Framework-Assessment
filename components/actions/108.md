# 108: Reconcile Rates & Track Completion

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 108-reconcile-rates

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Reconcile rates, discounts, and credits against a stated SLA and track completion.

## Supplemental Guidance

The description asks for reconciliation against a stated SLA rather than quickly, because quickly is
not a target anyone can be measured against. The target is yours to set, and the Framework expects
monthly validation as the baseline rhythm, so an SLA longer than the invoice cycle means the next
invoice arrives before the last one is settled.

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though most practices measure
completion time only after a reconciliation has slipped past a close.

Item 5 is the maturity marker. The Framework's Run tier has Finance
reconciling without the central team, which means the data, the method, and the access all have to be
usable by somebody who does not work in FinOps.

Action 107 scores the invoice reaching the ledger. This action scores whether what the invoice says is
correct. A practice can map every line perfectly and still be paying an unapplied discount.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every provider invoice received during the review period.

* Reconciliation cadence published (invoices are reconciled on a stated cadence with a named owner)
* Scope stated (reconciliation covers rates, discounts, and credits rather than the total alone)
* Completion measured against an SLA (the time from invoice receipt to reconciliation complete is
  measured against a stated target)
* Discrepancies recorded (each discrepancy carries its amount, its cause, and how it was resolved)
* Finance reconciles independently (Finance completes a reconciliation without the central FinOps team
  doing it for them)

10*Ceil(x/5)
