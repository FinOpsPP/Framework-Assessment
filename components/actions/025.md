# 25: Automate Alert Routing

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 25-automate-alert

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Automate alert routing to owners via existing channels (email/Slack/Teams).

## Supplemental Guidance

The denominator is every owner whose scope detection covers, not every owner who has ever received
an alert. A practice routing perfectly to the three teams it monitors, out of thirty teams running
spend, is not near 100%. It has a detection coverage problem that this percentage is meant to show.

Routed automatically is the distinction the capability draws between its Crawl and Walk tiers.
Alerts landing with a central team who then forward them are the Crawl state, however quickly the
forwarding happens, because the delay and the judgment both sit with a person.

Reaching the owner means reaching somebody who can act. An alert routed to a shared mailbox that
nobody owns counts the same as no alert, and the number will look fine while nothing happens.

Action 23 configures what gets detected and action 24 defines what happens next. This action
measures only how much of the organization the alerts reach. Action 28 measures how long the
notification takes once routing exists.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

x = Resource and budget owners who receive anomaly alerts for the scopes they own, routed
automatically
y = All owners whose scopes anomaly detection covers

x / y

### Scoring Details

**Score Type:** percent

| Score | Condition |
| ----- | --------- |
| 0 | No alerting to any owners |
| 1 | Alerting in at least one owner |
| 3 | Alerting to some owners [>20%] |
| 6 | Alerting to most owners [>60%] |
| 10 | Alerting to all owners [100%] |
