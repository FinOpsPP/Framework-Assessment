# 96: Automate Tasks & Review Maturity

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 96-automate-tasks

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Automate repetitive FinOps tasks; review the effect on team effort and cycle time.

## Supplemental Guidance

These are scored as a bucket because you can do them in any order, though ranking candidates without
an effort estimate tends to rank them by annoyance instead.

The description asks for the effect on team effort and cycle time rather than naming a review
frequency. How often a practice looks depends on how fast its toil changes, and fixing a cadence in
the description would score the calendar rather than the work.

Item 4 separates this from a backlog of automation ideas. An automation nobody measured may
have moved the work rather than removed it, which is common when a manual step becomes a manual
review of an automated step.

This action covers the practice's own repetitive work. Automating controls inside other capabilities
is scored where those controls live, such as action 14 for tagging enforcement, action 110 for
chargeback runs, and action 103 for guardrails. Reviewing capability maturity belongs to the FinOps
Assessment capability, where action 117 scores reassessment on a cadence.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: the tasks the practice performs more than once during the review period.

* Repeated tasks inventoried (the tasks the practice repeats are listed, with how often each runs)
* Effort recorded (each repeated task carries an estimate of the time it consumes)
* Automation candidates ranked (tasks are ordered by effort saved against cost to automate)
* Effect measured (time or cycle time before and after an automation is recorded)

10*Ceil(x/4)
