# 15: Iterate Strategies & Communicate

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 15-iterate-strategy

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Iterate strategies and communicate changes broadly.

## Supplemental Guidance

The steps are ordered on purpose. A change cannot be communicated before it is made, and adoption cannot
be measured before the change takes effect. A team that announces a taxonomy change without versioning it
leaves owners unable to tell which rules applied during a given period.

Item 4 is scored on reaching the owners a change binds, not on volume of communication. A change published
only to the FinOps team does not meet it.

This action closes the loop opened by actions 9 through 14. It does not re-score coverage or compliance
themselves, which are measured by actions 10, 12, and 13.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

1. Hold a recurring review of the allocation strategy on a published cadence with a named owner
2. Bring allocation measures into the review: unallocated cost, untagged cost, and tagging policy compliance
3. Version each change to metadata, hierarchy, or shared cost rules with its rationale and effective date
4. Communicate approved changes to the affected resource and budget owners before the effective date
5. Measure adoption after each change and carry the remaining gaps into the next review

10*Ceil(x/5)
