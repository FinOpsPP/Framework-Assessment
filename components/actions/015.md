# 15: Iterate Strategies & Communicate

**Specification Version:** 2.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

**Slug:** 15-iterate-strategy

**Implementation Types:**

* None

## Descriptive Statement

Iterate strategies and communicate changes broadly.

## Supplemental Guidance

A team can add change notices before it adds versioning, so the items are not preconditions for one
another and this is scored as a bucket rather than a sequential process. The word iterate suggests a
loop, which invites the wrong score type.

No coverage threshold is stated because the population is a handful of changes in a period. A percentage
rounds hard at that size: with three changes, one miss falls to 67%. An assessor enumerates the changes
instead of computing a rate.

Advance notice is met by reaching the owners a change binds, not by volume of communication. A change
published only to the FinOps team does not meet it.

Actions 10, 12, and 13 measure unallocated cost, tagging coverage, and compliance gaps. Those measures
are the inputs this review consumes, and they are scored there rather than here.

## Scoring Guide

**Weight:** 1.0

### Formula

Measured over: every change to metadata, hierarchy, or shared cost rules made during the review period.
Changes are few enough to enumerate, so items are assessed against each one rather than as a coverage
rate.

* Recurring review (the allocation strategy is reviewed on a published cadence with a named owner)
* Change record (every change carries a rationale and an effective date)
* Advance notice (every change reaches affected resource and budget owners before the effective date)
* Carried gaps (each review records the gaps it did not close, and the next review lists them)

10*Ceil(x/4)

### Scoring Details

**Score Type:** bucket

| Score | Condition |
| ----- | --------- |
| 0 | No items completed |
| 3 | 1 item completed |
| 5 | 2 items completed |
| 8 | 3 items completed |
| 10 | 4 items completed |
