# 4: Understand Usage & Cost

**Specification Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

## Descriptive Statement

Within this Domain, organizations work to gather all required information
 about their technology costs, usage, and other metrics; normalize it across
 data sources; define how it will be allocated among cost centers;
 and define reporting to make data available for use by
 all personas in this and in other Domains.
