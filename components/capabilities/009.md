# 9: Finops Assessment

**Specification Version:** 1.0.0

## Descriptive Statement

Conducting repeatable, measurable, thoughtful analysis of the maturity
 of a FinOps Practice in the context of the FinOps Framework for insights
 into strengths and areas for improvement of FinOps activities.
