# 2: Anomaly Management

**Specification Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Accepted

## Descriptive Statement

Detect, identify, alert and manage unexpected or un-forecasted technology cost
 and usage irregularities in a timely manner to lower risk
 and ensure cost-effective operations.
