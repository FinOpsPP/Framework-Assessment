# 6: Governance, Policy & Risk

**Specification Version:** 1.1.0

**Status:** Accepted

## Descriptive Statement

Establishing and evolving policies, controls, and governance mechanisms
 to ensure that technology use across every FinOps Scope aligns with
 business objectives, complies with regulatory requirements,
 and mitigates financial and operational risk.
